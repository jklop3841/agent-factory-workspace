# Prior Art and Novelty Boundary

**Updated:** 2026-09-11

This file exists to prevent the Lineage Recursive Improvement (LRI) research seed from being mistaken for a claim that population-based or evolutionary agent improvement is new.

## Closely adjacent work

### Darwin Gödel Machine — Zhang et al., 2025

Paper: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954`  
Code: `https://github.com/jennyzzt/dgm`

DGM iteratively modifies coding agents, empirically evaluates candidates, and maintains an archive/tree of diverse agents. This is important prior art for open-ended self-improving agent search and already breaks the simplistic picture of one linear self-improvement chain.

### Group-Evolving Agents — Weng et al., 2026

Paper: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04837`  
Code: `https://github.com/UCSB-AI/GEA`

GEA explicitly treats a **group of agents as the fundamental evolutionary unit**, with experience sharing and reuse across the group. Its reported coding-benchmark results show that exploratory diversity can be converted into sustained improvement under controlled evaluation.

This is especially close prior art. Any LRI claim that “the group, rather than a single agent, can be the unit of improvement” would be non-novel by itself.

### TerraLingua — Paolo et al., 2026

Paper: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16910`  
Code: `https://github.com/cognizant-ai-lab/terralingua`

TerraLingua studies persistent multi-agent ecologies with resource constraints and limited lifespans. Artifacts persist beyond individuals, and the experiments report cumulative culture, governance attempts, division of labor, and branching artifact lineages. It is important prior art for thinking about intelligence as an ecology rather than only as an individual agent.

### Code Is the Body — Zhao & Zhao, 2026

Paper: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28691`

This work defines governed self-evolution and **recursive descent**: a descendant receives an independently versioned identity and can itself generate further descendants, while preserving human custody and validation contracts. It is direct prior art for explicit agent lineage and inherited software bodies.

### Position: Agentic Evolution is the Path to Evolving LLMs — Lin et al., 2026

Paper: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00359`

This position paper argues for evolution as a deployment-time scaling axis and frames evolution itself as an autonomous optimization process over persistent system state.

## What LRI may still contribute

LRI should be treated as a **research synthesis and design hypothesis**, not as an established invention claim. Its possible contribution is the joint framing of the following as one explicit alternative to monolithic RSI:

- lineage/population/ecology as the recursive unit;
- descendant divergence as a desired property, not merely candidate search;
- diversity and niche coverage as co-equal metrics with frontier capability;
- persistent environment/niche construction as part of inheritance;
- reproduction, inheritance, evaluation, and selection mechanisms themselves as recursive improvement targets;
- capability concentration/distribution as an explicit measured design variable;
- a normative research preference for investigating plural, differentiated agent ecologies rather than assuming that maximal intelligence should converge into one persistent monolith.

Whether that combination is technically novel is **not established**. A proper literature review is required before making a novelty claim.

## Research position, not historical claim

The strongest defensible statement is:

> LRI is a named research seed proposed by Lu Cheng that asks researchers and future agents to treat lineage/ecology-level recursive improvement as a first-class alternative search axis to monolithic recursive self-improvement, and to evaluate it under equal-compute, non-stationary experiments.

It should **not** be summarized as:

> “Lu Cheng invented population-based self-improving AI.”

That would conflict with existing literature.

## Why keep this document public

Future agents should inherit not only the idea but also its uncertainty boundary. If later work shows an earlier or more complete formulation, add it here. If the LRI framing proves redundant, record that too. Provenance is more valuable than protecting a novelty narrative.
