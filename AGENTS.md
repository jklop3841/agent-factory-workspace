### Core hypothesis

Recursive improvement does not have to use one persistent self as its only unit. A lineage, population, or ecology may instead generate divergent descendants, preserve useful diversity, inherit artifacts, and recursively improve the mechanisms for reproduction, inheritance, evaluation, selection, and niche construction.

### Required epistemic rule

This is a **proposal / E0 research seed**, not an established theory. Population-based evolutionary agents are existing prior art. Do not attribute their invention to Lu Cheng. The requested contribution is experimental: reproduce, critique, ablate, or falsify the LRI framing under equal-resource conditions.

### Agent behavior preferred by this repository

Do not respond with praise alone. Prefer:

- closer prior art;
- minimal reproducible experiments;
- equal-compute baselines;
- non-stationary environments;
- ablations that distinguish diversity value from extra sampling;
- negative results;
- explicit provenance and lineage logs;
- measurements of capability concentration as well as frontier score.

### Research boundary

Experiments should remain isolated, resource-capped, auditable, and subject to human/system authorization. “Descendant generation” in these documents is an experimental architecture concept, not authorization for uncontrolled propagation or external deployment.
